## Limitations and next steps

1. **Synthetic internal data and stand-in weather** (A1, A2). Rules and models are validated against known truth,
   but business magnitudes are illustrative.
   **Next:** run `fetch_public_data.py` for NASA POWER, then pilot on a de-identified PAS extract under GBHS
   governance approval.
2. **Power BI artefacts are not yet produced in Desktop.** The PBIP is schema-valid and TOM-validated. Creating the
   `.pbix` and `.pbit`, verifying the DAX at execution level, taking screenshots and recording Desktop timings all
   need Windows; the steps are scripted in `BUILD_GUIDE.md`.
3. **Observational analytics.** The wait drivers and readmission odds ratios are associations.
   **Next:** a stepped roll-out of the seasonal roster uplift (facility by facility), evaluated with the same
   difference-in-differences design.
4. **Readmissions to other providers are invisible.** **Next:** link to MoHSS hospital data through a governed
   pseudonymised key.
5. **Early warning lead time is only 2–6 weeks.** **Next:** rainfall-triggered thresholds, and forecasts by
   facility and by entomological indicators.
6. **Operationalisation.** **Next:**
   - schedule `run_all.py` (pg_cron or Task Scheduler) with alerts on any WARN or FAIL;
   - deploy to a Pro workspace with the gateway, app audiences and Purview labels;
   - carry out the quarterly access reviews.
