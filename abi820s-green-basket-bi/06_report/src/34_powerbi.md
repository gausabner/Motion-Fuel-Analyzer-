# 3.4 Power BI semantic model

The semantic model `GreenBasket_Agri.SemanticModel` is delivered as a **Power BI Project (PBIP)**: the model is
stored in TMDL and the report in PBIR (Microsoft, 2024a, 2024d). The definition files use format version 4.0
(Microsoft Fabric Community, 2025). It is defined in one place, `04_powerbi/model_spec.py`,
and generated by `04_powerbi/build_pbip.py`. Before release it was validated three ways:

- **Microsoft's Tabular Object Model** deserialised and re-serialised it in Power BI compatibility mode (round trip OK).
- **All 58 report files** passed the official PBIR JSON schemas.
- **A static DAX check** confirmed that every measure, column and table reference resolves.

## Storage mode: Import, with incremental refresh

<!-- table: Storage-mode decision -->

| Option | Assessment for Green Basket | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Import** | 303 k sales lines plus 8 smaller facts compress to about 11 MB in VertiPaq (DuckDB columnar proxy, §3.7), far below the 1 GB Pro limit. Every visual is answered from memory in milliseconds. Full DAX and time intelligence. Data freshness is daily, matching the sources (farm logs and ERP extracts are daily). | **Chosen** |
| DirectQuery | Every visual becomes SQL through the on-premises gateway to PostgreSQL. Latency adds up across 6–10 visuals per page. Load lands on the operational database. Some DAX patterns are slower. There is no real-time need. | Rejected |
| Composite (Dual + DirectQuery detail) | Useful once invoice detail reaches tens of millions of rows. Import the aggregate `mart.agg_sales_monthly` and query the detail by DirectQuery through user-defined aggregations. The aggregate already exists (§3.7), so this is a ready growth path. | Future option |
| Direct Lake | Needs Microsoft Fabric (OneLake Delta tables). Not in the division's licensing. | Not applicable |

**Incremental refresh.** The `Sales` query filters on the integer invoice date key between the `RangeStart` and
`RangeEnd` parameters. The filter is written so that it folds to PostgreSQL:

```
Int64.From(DateTime.ToText(RangeStart, "yyyyMMdd"))
```

The policy archives 5 years and refreshes the last 10 days. A daily refresh then re-reads about 2,200 rows instead of
302,903 (§3.7).

## Relationships

All relationships are **many-to-one** from fact to dimension, with **single-direction** cross-filtering. There are no
bidirectional or many-to-many relationships, which avoids ambiguous filter paths and keeps RLS propagation
predictable. Role-playing dates are inactive relationships, activated inside measures with `USERELATIONSHIP`:

- Sales: invoice date (active) and dispatch date.
- Crop Cycle: planting date (active), first harvest, last harvest, and season end. Season end is used by every
  yield measure, so yield conforms to the NSA season.

`Security User Zone` is deliberately **disconnected**. It is read only by the RLS expression.

## Model hygiene

- **Hidden technical columns.** 93 of 198 columns are hidden:
  - all surrogate and foreign keys;
  - sort keys;
  - the raw numeric columns behind measures.

  Users therefore see only business attributes and measures. **Implicit measures are disabled**
  (`discourageImplicitMeasures`), so every number on a page comes from a governed DAX measure.
- **Display folders.**
  - Date columns sit in *Calendar*, *Fiscal (Apr–Mar)* and *Agricultural season (Oct–Sep)*.
  - Farm and Customer separate *As-is (current)* attributes from *SCD history*.
  - Measures sit in a dedicated `_Measures` table in folders *0 Labels* to *7 Data Quality & Security*.
- **Sort-by-column.** Six settings, for example:
  - Month Name by Month Number;
  - Fiscal Month (Apr = 1) by Fiscal Month Number;
  - Year Month by Year Month Key;
  - Day Name by ISO day of week.

  These make April the first month of the fiscal year in every visual.
- **Hierarchies.** Fiscal Calendar (FY › FQ › Month › Date), Calendar, Agricultural Season (Season › Month › Date)
  and Geography (Zone › Region).
- **Data categories.** Region is *State or Province*; Town is *City*; farm coordinates are *Latitude/Longitude*
  for map visuals.
- **Date table.** `Date` is marked as the date table, and Auto date/time is switched off
  (`__PBI_TimeIntelligenceEnabled = 0`), which avoids dozens of hidden date tables.
- **Format strings.** `"N$" #,0` for currency and `0.0%` for ratios, set on the measures themselves.

## DAX measures

There are 79 measures in total; Appendix B lists all of them, generated from `model_spec.py`. The fourteen below cover
every required pattern:

- time intelligence: YTD, YoY and rolling averages;
- `CALCULATE` with filter modification: `KEEPFILTERS`, `REMOVEFILTERS`, `USERELATIONSHIP`, and a `TOPN` table filter;
- a semi-additive balance;
- a drill-across benchmark.

**Fiscal year to date** uses year end `"3/31"`. The comparison with last year is **like-for-like**: it stops at the
same day last year as the latest data date. Without that cut-off, a partial FY2027 would be compared with a
complete FY2026 and show a false −73 %.

<!-- dax: Net Revenue; Gross Margin %; Last Sales Date; Net Revenue FYTD Raw; Net Revenue FYTD; Net Revenue FYTD PY; Net Revenue FYTD YoY % -->

**Prior year, year-on-year and rolling measures.** The rolling measures stop at the last data date, so the trailing
months are not half-empty.

<!-- dax: Net Revenue PY; Net Revenue YoY %; Net Revenue MAT; Net Revenue 3M Rolling Avg -->

**`CALCULATE` with filter-context modification**:

- `KEEPFILTERS` intersects with the user's filters instead of replacing them, so Export Share stays correct when the
  user also slices by channel.
- `REMOVEFILTERS` creates a share of the grand total.
- `TOPN` builds a table filter.
- `USERELATIONSHIP` switches the date role.

<!-- dax: Export Revenue; Export Share %; Revenue Share of All Crops %; Top 10 Customer Share %; Dispatched Volume (t) -->

**Yield, the benchmark and the semi-additive stock balance.**

- **Yield** uses the season-end date role, so it conforms to the NSA estimates.
- **The benchmark** drills across to `Crop Estimate` through the shared Region, Crop and Date dimensions.
- **Closing stock** takes the last snapshot in the period. It is never summed over months; the SQL proof that the
  sum would be wrong is in §3.5.

<!-- dax: Completed Harvest (t); Yield (t/ha); Benchmark Yield (t/ha); Closing Stock (t); Stock Cover (days) -->

**Field parameter.** `Metric Selector` is a field parameter over six measures:

- Net Revenue
- Gross Margin
- Gross Margin %
- Sales Volume (t)
- Avg Realised Price (N$/kg)
- Export Share %

One trend visual can then answer six questions. A slicer on `'Metric Selector'[Metric]` switches the measure
without duplicating visuals. This is a non-technical user's self-service entry point (§3.6).

**Dynamic RLS design.**

- The mapping of user to zone lives in the warehouse (`sec.user_zone_access`). The data owner maintains it there,
  so access changes need no model republish.
- The model imports the mapping as the hidden, disconnected table `Security User Zone`.
- The *Zone Manager* filter on `Farm` keeps only farms whose management zone is mapped to `USERPRINCIPALNAME()`.
- Filters flow from Farm to all six farm-linked facts: sales, harvest, crop cycle, cost, inventory and weather.
- One user can hold several zones. `ops.north` holds North-Central and North-East.
- An unmapped user sees **nothing**, so the role fails closed.

**Test plan and results.** The expected values were computed independently in SQL (query `ev_rls_expected` in
`12_evidence_queries.sql`). Each role is then checked in Power BI Desktop with **Modeling › View as › Other user +
role**, and after publishing with **Security › Test as role**.

**Reading the test.**

- The four zone totals add up to N$ 451,947,728.
- The N$ 6,825,707 difference is revenue on the *Unknown* farm (rule SA05). Only executives see it, by design.
- The page-4 card *Farms visible (RLS)* (`[Visible Farms]`) and *Signed-in user* (`[Current User]`) make every test
  self-documenting in a screenshot.

**Security limits that the team must know.**

- **RLS restricts *Viewers* only.** Workspace Admins, Members and Contributors bypass it, so zone managers consume
  the content through the **app** with Viewer rights (§3.8).
- **Dimension members are not filtered.** Zone managers can still see the *names* of customers outside their zone in
  a slicer, although with no revenue. This is accepted for a B2B customer list. If it became sensitive, the remedy
  is "apply security filter in both directions" on Sales→Customer, at a measured performance cost, or object-level
  security on `Customer`.
- **RLS is not a substitute for a data-sharing decision.** The Executive role holds all data, so its membership is
  limited to the executive committee and the CFO, through an Entra ID group.
