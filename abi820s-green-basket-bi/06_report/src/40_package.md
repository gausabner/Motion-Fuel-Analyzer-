## Limitations and next steps

1. **Synthetic internal data and stand-in external files** (A1, A2).
   - The pipeline and analytics are validated against known truth, but business magnitudes are illustrative.
   - **Next:** load the real NASA POWER, World Bank and FRED files with `fetch_public_data.py`, and transcribe the
     NSA/MAFWLR tables.
2. **Power BI artefacts not yet produced in Desktop.** The PBIP is schema-valid, but `.pbix`/`.pbit` creation,
   execution-level DAX verification, screenshots and Desktop timings require Windows. The steps are scripted in
   `BUILD_GUIDE.md`.
3. **Forecast validation uses one hold-out season.** **Next:** rolling-origin evaluation and exogenous drivers
   (FX, EU grape prices).
4. **Fertiliser measured at cycle level** from ledger allocations, which attenuates its estimated effect.
   **Next:** record input applications per block in the planting register.
5. **Yield effects are observational.** **Next:** paired-block trials when a farm converts to irrigation, using the
   SCD2 history to compare before and after.
6. **Operationalisation.** **Next:**
   - schedule `run_all.py` with `pg_cron` or Task Scheduler;
   - alert on any reconciliation WARN or FAIL;
   - deploy to a Pro workspace with the gateway, app audiences and Purview labels.
