## Design specificity verdict
The redesigned pages are authored for this product: the briefing sentence, the rule explanations and the stated data limits could not be lifted into another tool. The app as a whole is two products under one menu, because Fuel issues, Fuel receipts, Fuel logs, Reports, Uploads, Users and the lower half of Fleet still follow the old patterns.

Deterministic scan: 23 advisory findings, no errors. 15 radius values and 6 colours not in DESIGN.md (mostly pills, hairlines and Recharts tooltip alphas; one real hard-coded colour, #B8C4D8 at app/globals.css:316), and 2 notes on the deliberate spring curve. Overlay injection was blocked by the app's own Content Security Policy, so there is no in-browser overlay.

## What's working
1. Honesty is designed in: the pages say what the data cannot support instead of estimating.
2. The vehicle drawer is a real investigation tool with correct dialog semantics and focus handling.
3. Exceptions are reachable from everywhere: menu badges, the dashboard card, the briefing link, the registry backlog link.

## Priority issues
- [P1] The same quantity shows different values on different screens. Litres issued 2,224,769 (Fuel issues, Fleet) vs 2,155,031 (Reports); issue cost N$46,347,692 vs N$45,009,246; litres received 1,912,441 (Issues vs receipts) vs 658,876 (Fuel receipts); "Fuel spend" means received on one page and issued on another; 10 departments (Registry) vs 42 (Cost centres). Causes not yet traced. Fix: one shared totals source and label any intended subset. Command: /impeccable harden.
- [P1] Two period systems with one label. "This month" is the latest month with data on Dashboard and Exceptions, and the calendar month on every filter-bar page (probably empty). Default scope flips between one month and all time. Fix: one period control everywhere, carried in the URL, with the range printed in the header. Command: /impeccable clarify.
- [P1] Destructive and security actions lack protection. Registry removals have no confirm or Undo; password reset uses prompt() with the password visible; delete user uses confirm(); role change and disable fire instantly; the role select has no accessible name. Fix: Undo toast on removals, app dialogs with a masked field, aria-labels. Command: /impeccable harden.
- [P2] Legacy pages ignore the design system: no summary sentence, title-case labels, unformatted numbers and dates, unit numbers that are not plates, "Hudson" still named on Uploads, five duplicated legacy charts on Fleet. Command: /impeccable polish.
- [P2] Mobile and contrast. /dashboard/exceptions scrolls sideways at 375px (the screen-reader-only "Actions" header in ExceptionsTable.tsx:61 escapes its wrapper); /dashboard/fuel-logs likewise. Status pills sit just under AA (4.39 and 4.46 against 4.5); the petrol legend and "29.0%" label are 2.54; chart diamonds are 11 to 16px touch targets. Command: /impeccable adapt.

## Persona red flags
